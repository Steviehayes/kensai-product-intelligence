I put three popular options head to head: Ritual Essential for Women,
Vitabiotics Wellwoman Original, and MoleQlar's Vitamin B Komplex.

- Ritual: sleek, genuinely vegan, nice methylated forms. Note: when I first
  reviewed Ritual's OLDER formulation it listed around 320 mg DHA — they've
  tweaked the formula since, so double-check the current label.
- Wellwoman: great all-rounder BUT the capsule is gelatin, so it is NOT
  suitable for vegans or vegetarians despite what some shops tag it as.
- MoleQlar: strong bioactive B-complex, clearly vegan.

One frustration: I could not find an official EU or UK price for Ritual
anywhere — it seems US-only for now.

(Prices and formulas change — I don't always update these posts.)
